Who runs this

A directory for independent filmmakers, with a documentary niche.

Documentary411 is not a watch catalog and not a generic film-school listicle. It is a working directory of grants, festivals, fiscal sponsors, markets, legal resources, gear, and practical tools — built for independent filmmakers, with documentary as the specialty.

Identity

Kerry David · KDC and Films

Documentary411: Founded by Kerry David, KDC and Films. Contact: admin@kdcandfilms.com.

Kerry is a 25-year film veteran. Credits and campaigns associated with that work have earned her projects, 75+ film festival awards and over $90 million in cumulative box office receipts.

Profiles: IMDb · LinkedIn.

The site exists for independent filmmakers like Kerry, to have a secure place to verify grants, funds, festivals, fiscal sponsors, filmmaker services and markets. Listings carry a last-verified date and a correction link. Deadlines change, funds come and go; we expect filmmakers to confirm on the official page before applying.
