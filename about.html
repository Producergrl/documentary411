Who runs this

A directory for independent filmmakers, with a documentary niche.

Documentary411 is not a watch catalog and not a generic film-school listicle. It is a working directory of grants, festivals, fiscal sponsors, markets, legal resources, gear, and practical tools — built for independent filmmakers, with documentary as the specialty.

Identity

Kerry David · KDC and Films

Documentary411 is created and maintained by Kerry David of KDC and Films. Contact: admin@kdcandfilms.com.

Kerry is a 25-year film veteran. Credits and campaigns associated with that work have earned 75+ film festival awards and about $90 million in cumulative box office, and Kerry has worked with most traditional distributors. Those numbers are the credential behind the directory — not a claim that Documentary411 is a streaming service or a film school.

The site exists so independent filmmakers have one place to verify grants, festivals, fiscal sponsors, and markets without treating member directories, cash grants, and festival submissions as the same thing. Listings carry a last-verified date and a correction link. Deadlines change; we expect filmmakers to confirm on the official page before applying.

Questions, corrections, or press: admin@kdcandfilms.com.
