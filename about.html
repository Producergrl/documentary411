Who runs this

A resource hub for filmmakers, run by a filmmaker.

Documentary411 is not a streaming catalog. It is a working directory of grants, festivals, fiscal sponsors, legal resources, gear, and paid strategy tools for independent documentary filmmakers.

Identity

Kerry David · KDC and Films

Documentary411 is created and maintained by Kerry David of KDC and Films. The site exists to cut through noisy grant lists and festival spray-and-pray advice with dated verification, honest open/closed status, and tools filmmakers actually use — including a festival cost calculator and a free Festival Budget Workbook.

Listings are a starting point, not gospel. Deadlines, fees, and eligibility change. Every resource card includes a last-verified date and a correction link so filmmakers can help keep the directory current.

Questions, corrections, or press: admin@kdcandfilms.com.
