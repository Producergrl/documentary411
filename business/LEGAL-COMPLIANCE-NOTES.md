# Legal & Compliance Notes — Revenue Sprint

**This is not legal advice.** These are working-language notes and flags; anything marked
"review" should go to an actual attorney before you rely on it, especially around the
19,500-contact list and any refund/guarantee language.

## Email marketing (CAN-SPAM, and CASL if any Canadian contacts)

- Only email contacts with a legitimate opt-in or existing business relationship. A
  25-year industry list built from real professional contact (production credits,
  conferences, past clients/students, newsletter opt-ins) generally has a defensible
  relationship basis under CAN-SPAM, but **CASL (Canada) requires express or clearly
  implied consent** — if the list includes Canadian contacts, segment and treat more
  conservatively, or exclude them from cold-style sends.
- Every commercial email needs: accurate sender info, a non-deceptive subject line, a
  working unsubscribe mechanism, and your business address. Confirm your ESP adds these
  automatically — most do, but verify before the first send.
- Honor unsubscribes/bounces already recorded against this list. Do not re-add anyone
  who previously opted out.
- **Review before sending:** if any portion of the 19,500 list came from a source where
  commercial email wasn't clearly anticipated (e.g., a list purchased or scraped rather
  than built through direct relationship), exclude that portion or get it reviewed —
  this is the single biggest risk in the whole plan and worth 30 minutes of founder
  time to confirm before Day 1.

## Testimonials & earnings claims

- The existing "Honesty Box" language on Funding Sprint and Funding Lab pages (no
  funding/sponsor outcome guaranteed) is the right pattern — **keep using it** in all
  new outreach copy. Do not let sales urgency water this down; it's both the ethical
  baseline and the FTC-compliant one (no implied income/outcome guarantee).
- Any future testimonials used in marketing need the standard FTC disclosure if
  compensated, and shouldn't imply typical/guaranteed results.

## Refunds

- Sprint: deposit refundable until the positioning call happens (already documented on
  the page) — keep enforcing exactly that line consistently across every sale, don't
  negotiate it case-by-case without updating the written policy everywhere.
- Festival Strategy / Ask a Pro: no refund policy currently visible on the pages
  reviewed — **review**: add an explicit, simple policy (e.g., "digital product, no
  refunds after download" for Festival Strategy; a clear no-show/reschedule policy for
  Consult) so support decisions aren't ad hoc.

## Tax

- Sales tax/VAT collection on digital products varies by buyer state/country. Confirm
  Stripe Tax (or equivalent) is configured, or that you have a documented reason it
  isn't required for your situation — **review with an accountant**, not covered here.

## Business registration

- Checkout copy references `admin@kdcandfilms.com`, suggesting sales may run through an
  existing business entity. Confirm invoicing/1099s and any sales-tax registration are
  aligned to whichever entity actually receives the Stripe payouts — **review**.

## Advertise offer (B2B)

- Treat advertiser agreements as month-to-month with clear cancellation terms in writing
  (email confirmation is sufficient at this volume) before charging a card.
