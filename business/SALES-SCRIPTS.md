# Sales Scripts & Objection Handling

## Funding Sprint ($2,500) — application review / positioning call

**Call structure (60 min, per existing offer terms):**
1. (5 min) Reconnect, confirm project name/stage from their Tally application
2. (15 min) Discovery questions (below)
3. (25 min) Reflect back their positioning gap — what's missing between where the film
   is and "sponsor-ready"; this is where the value of the Sprint becomes obvious, not
   asserted
4. (10 min) Walk the 10-day timeline and deliverables list from `/funding-sprint`
5. (5 min) Payment: confirm deposit ($1,250 or the 2× $1,350 split), send Stripe link,
   confirm intake questionnaire delivery

**Discovery questions:**
- Where is the film right now — picture lock, rough cut, still shooting?
- Who is the actual audience for this film, in one sentence?
- Have you approached any brands or sponsors already? What happened?
- What's your target premiere or release window?
- What would "sponsor-ready" actually change for you in the next 90 days?

**Objection: "$2,500 is a lot for a small indie doc."**
> Totally fair — it's priced for what it replaces: a positioning consultant, a deck
> designer, and weeks of your own research into which of the 60+ brand categories
> actually fit your film. Most filmmakers spend more than that in time alone getting
> it wrong twice before getting it right once. If the budget genuinely isn't there
> right now, the $297 self-guided system covers the same method — I'd rather point
> you there than have you stretch for the Sprint. *(Note: only offer this if Funding
> Lab checkout has been reopened — until then, don't offer $297 as a live alternative.)*

**Objection: "Will this actually get me a sponsor?"**
> No — and I'd be lying if I said yes. What's guaranteed is the deliverables: the
> positioning, the deck, the 25-brand list, the outreach sequence, built to spec. The
> follow-through and the film are yours. That's in writing because it's true, not
> because a lawyer made me say it.

**Objection: "Can I think about it?"**
> Of course. Two things worth knowing while you decide: we cap at four films a month,
> and the deposit is fully refundable until the positioning call actually happens — so
> there's no real risk in locking a slot while you're still deciding.

## Ask a Pro Consult ($500) — pre-call framing (sent after purchase, via intake confirmation)

Already has a working intake form. Add this note in the confirmation email:

> Before our call, take 10 minutes to write down the single decision or problem you
> most want to leave clearer about. Sessions that start with one specific question go
> further than ones that try to cover everything.

**Objection (pre-purchase, in reply to inbound question): "What's the difference between
the $50 question and the $500 consult?"**
> The $50 is one focused written or short-call answer to one specific question. The
> $500 hour is a working session — we can go through your deck, your budget, your
> festival list, or your outreach plan together in real time and leave with an actual
> plan, not just an answer.

## Festival Strategy ($99) — objection handling for inbound replies

**"Is this specific to documentaries or all indie film?"**
> Built by and for documentary/indie producers specifically, including doc-specific
> pieces like impact-release strategy, but the festival-run mechanics apply broadly.

**"I already know festival strategy basics."**
> Fair — this isn't the basics. It's the day-by-day operating system: the six-factor
> scorecard for deciding which festivals are worth the fee, the three-touch press
> sequence, and the full budget worksheet. If you already have all of that built and
> working, you don't need it.
