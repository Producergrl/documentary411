# Documentary411 — $10K / 30-Day Revenue Sprint

This folder is the working plan for turning Documentary411's already-live offers into
$10,000 in collected revenue within 30 days, using direct outreach and the existing
~19,500-contact network — no paid ads, no new product build.

**Start here:** `DECISION-LOG.md` explains why this sprint targets the existing platform
instead of a new company, and what's assumption vs. evidence.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `DECISION-LOG.md` | Founder-asset audit, opportunity scoring, the core decision, assumptions register, kill/revise/proceed thresholds |
| `REVENUE-MODEL.md` | The $10K math: offer mix, funnel assumptions, best/probable/weak-case scenarios |
| `30-DAY-REVENUE-SPRINT.md` | Day-by-day plan: objectives, who does what (founder vs. AI), decision points |
| `EMAIL-CAMPAIGNS.md` | Ready-to-send outreach copy for each list segment, pointing at real live checkout links |
| `SALES-SCRIPTS.md` | Call structure, discovery questions, and objection handling for each offer |
| `DASHBOARD.md` | Daily/weekly KPI tracking template with pace benchmarks |
| `PROSPECT-TRACKER.csv` | Row-per-contact outreach tracker |
| `LEGAL-COMPLIANCE-NOTES.md` | Email compliance, refund policy gaps, and other items flagged for review — not legal advice |

## What this sprint deliberately does NOT do

- Does not touch the paused Funding Lab ($297 course) checkout — it's incomplete, and
  selling it would contradict the site's own no-guarantee/honesty standard.
- Does not send any email — all campaign copy is drafted and ready, but sending requires
  the founder's list/ESP access and judgment about consent, which this session doesn't have.
- Does not invent a new brand, name, or company — Documentary411's existing positioning,
  credibility copy, and Stripe infrastructure are reused as-is.

## Immediate next action (founder, ~30 minutes)

1. Test all three live checkout links yourself (see Day 1 in the sprint plan).
2. Confirm your list's segmentation and consent status in your ESP.
3. Send the first Campaign 1 batch to 25–35 warm contacts.
