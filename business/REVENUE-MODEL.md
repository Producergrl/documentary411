## Funnel math behind each line

Assume the 19,500-contact list segments roughly as:
- **~700 "warm/high-value"** contacts: past collaborators, known active filmmakers/producers,
  people who've had direct contact with the founder (candidates for Funding Sprint + Consult,
  approached mostly 1:1, not blast email)
- **~6,000 "relevant/engaged"**: filmmakers plausibly in production, post, or festival-prep
  stage (candidates for the Festival Strategy blast and general Ask-a-Pro offer)
- **~12,800 "unclassified/cold"**: not prioritized in the first 30 days — used only for a
  single light-touch re-engagement send, not aggressive selling

*(These splits are assumptions — see `DECISION-LOG.md` §5. Replace with real ESP segment
counts on Day 1.)*

**Funding Sprint (2 sales target from 4 slots):**
70 warm contacts approached 1:1 → ~20 replies (30%) → ~8 qualify as having a near-complete
or completed film → ~5 apply → 2–3 close after the positioning call. This is a relationship-sales
motion, not a mass-email motion — it needs direct, personalized outreach from the founder, not a blast.

**Ask a Pro Consult (8 sales target):**
Combine warm-list direct asks (part of the same 70–150 personal touches above) with a
dedicated email to the 6,000 "relevant" segment. At a conservative 0.15–0.3% conversion
from a well-targeted email to a $500 service, that segment alone can plausibly produce
9–18 consult sales; targeting 8 is conservative.

**Festival Strategy (15–25 sales target):**
Lowest-friction, instant-delivery, lowest price. A dedicated send to the ~6,000 "relevant"
segment at a 0.25–0.5% conversion (realistic for a warm B2B list buying a $99 self-serve
tool, higher than cold-list benchmarks) yields 15–30 sales. This is the volume/resilience
line of the model — the offer most likely to close the gap if the two higher-ticket
lines underperform.

## Scenarios

| Scenario | Sprint | Consult | Question | Festival Strategy | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Best case** | 4 ($10,000) | 12 ($6,000) | 15 ($750) | 30 ($2,970) | **$19,720** |
| **Probable case** | 2 ($5,000) | 8 ($4,000) | 10 ($500) | 15 ($1,485) | **$10,985** |
| **Weak case** | 1 ($2,500) | 4 ($2,000) | 5 ($250) | 15 ($1,485) | **$6,235** |

**Candor:** the weak case falls short of $10,000. The gap is almost entirely the Funding
Sprint line — it is the highest-value but highest-friction offer (a $2,500 application-gated
service), and its close rate depends on the quality of the ~700 warm contacts and how many
have a film in a fundable state *right now*. If Day 10 shows fewer than 3 Sprint applications,
the plan should shift weight toward Consult and Festival Strategy volume rather than waiting
on Sprint deals to materialize (see pivot rules in `30-DAY-REVENUE-SPRINT.md`).

## What is deliberately excluded from the $10K math

- Funding Lab ($297 course) — checkout intentionally paused; not counted.
- Advertise revenue — real and pursued in parallel (see plan), but B2B sponsor sales cycles
  typically run longer than 30 days from cold, so it's treated as upside, not core math.
- Affiliate/equipment shop revenue — not controllable or estimable; excluded.

## Cash-collection timing

- Festival Strategy, Ask-a-Pro Question, Ask-a-Pro Consult: card charged at checkout — cash
  same day as the sale.
- Funding Sprint: 50% deposit at application approval, 50% on delivery (day ~10 of that
  engagement) — so a Sprint sold on Day 12 collects its second half around Day 22, still
  inside the 30-day window if sold early enough. **Sprints must be sold by Day 15–18** to
  fully collect within 30 days; sell later and only the deposit counts toward the 30-day total.

## Refund/cancellation assumption

Existing copy already sets expectations correctly (no funding outcome guaranteed, deliverables
guaranteed instead). Model assumes a 5% refund/chargeback rate on Consult and Festival Strategy,
0 on Sprint (deposit refundable only pre-positioning-call, per existing terms).
