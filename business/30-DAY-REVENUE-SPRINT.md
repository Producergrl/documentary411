# 30-Day Execution Plan — Documentary411 Revenue Sprint

Selling starts before anything is "perfected." All copy referenced already exists in
`EMAIL-CAMPAIGNS.md` and `SALES-SCRIPTS.md`. Founder = Kerry David. AI = tasks completable
without founder credentials, contacts, or judgment calls only the founder can make.

## Week 1 (Days 1–7): Verify, segment, first sends, first sale attempt

**Day 1 — Objective: confirm the machine actually works**
- Founder: personally test all 3 checkout links end-to-end (Festival Strategy, Ask a Pro
  $50, Ask a Pro $500) with a real card, confirm PDF delivery and Netlify form intake work.
- Founder: confirm ESP access and export/segment the 19,500 list per the 3-tier split in
  `REVENUE-MODEL.md` (warm / relevant / cold) using whatever tags/history you actually have.
- Founder: confirm Sprint capacity — how many of the 4 monthly slots are genuinely open.
- AI: none (requires account access only the founder has).
- Decision threshold: if any checkout is broken, stop and fix before Day 2 — no traffic to
  a broken link.

**Day 2 — Objective: warm list outreach begins**
- Founder: send Campaign 1 (1:1, personalized) to the first 25–35 warm contacts.
- AI: draft personalized variants of Campaign 1 for named contacts if founder supplies
  names/context.
- Metric: sends logged in `PROSPECT-TRACKER.csv`.

**Day 3 — Objective: broad Festival Strategy send**
- Founder: send Campaign 2 to the "relevant" segment (or a first batch if segmenting
  gradually to protect deliverability).
- AI: prep Campaign 3 (Ask a Pro) send-ready for Day 5.
- Metric: opens/clicks logged by end of day.

**Day 4 — Objective: follow up + first replies handled**
- Founder: send Campaign 1 follow-up to Day-2 non-repliers; respond personally to any
  replies (this is where trust and eventual Sprint/Consult sales are actually won).
- AI: draft reply templates for common responses (interested / not now / wrong fit) for
  founder to personalize and send.
- Decision threshold: if reply rate to Campaign 1 is <10%, revise the message (see
  Pivot Rules) before Day 6, not after Week 2.

**Day 5 — Objective: second broad send, second warm batch**
- Founder: send Campaign 3 (Ask a Pro) to the general list; send Campaign 1 to next 25–35
  warm contacts.
- AI: update dashboard with Week 1 running totals.

**Day 6–7 — Objective: first close, Week 1 review**
- Founder: aim for at least 1 paid close by end of Day 7 (most likely Festival Strategy or
  a $50 question — fastest-close offers).
- Founder + AI: fill Week 1 rollup in `DASHBOARD.md`, compare to benchmark ($1,500
  cumulative by Day 7).
- Decision: if behind benchmark, diagnose using the framework in Pivot Rules below before
  Week 2 sends go out.

## Week 2 (Days 8–14): Scale what worked, start Sprint calls

**Day 8** — Objective: apply Week 1 learnings. Founder + AI revise whichever campaign
underperformed (see Pivot Rules); resend improved version to a fresh slice of the same segment.

**Day 9** — Objective: warm batch 3 (next 25–35 contacts) + handle any Sprint applications
that came in — schedule positioning calls this week.

**Day 10** — Objective: Campaign 4 (cold re-engagement) sent to a first slice of the cold
segment — treat as list-hygiene + upside, not core-plan revenue.

**Day 11** — Objective: first Funding Sprint positioning call(s), using the script in
`SALES-SCRIPTS.md`. Founder only — this call cannot be delegated.

**Day 12** — Objective: warm batch 4; follow up on any outstanding proposals/replies.

**Day 13–14** — Objective: Week 2 review. Benchmark: $4,500 cumulative by Day 14.
Decision: if fewer than 1 Sprint application has come in by Day 14, treat Sprint as
"revise" per Pivot Rules — the $2,500 line is unlikely to recover in the second half
without a message or targeting change.

## Week 3 (Days 15–21): Last window to sell Sprints for full 30-day collection

**Day 15** — **Hard deadline for new Sprint sales** if full payment (deposit + balance) is
to land within the 30-day window (10 business days delivery + close-out). Founder:
push remaining warm-list outreach specifically toward Sprint-qualified contacts.

**Day 16–17** — Objective: second Festival Strategy send (new subject/angle, from
`EMAIL-CAMPAIGNS.md` follow-up variant) to segment members who didn't open the first send.

**Day 18** — Objective: Ask a Pro push — direct outreach to anyone who engaged with earlier
sends but didn't buy; personal note, not another blast.

**Day 19–20** — Objective: referral ask. Founder emails/messages anyone who has already
bought anything this month: "know one other filmmaker who'd get value from this?" — zero
build cost, highest-trust channel available.

**Day 21** — Objective: Week 3 review. Benchmark: $8,000 cumulative.
Decision: if total is materially behind, shift all remaining effort to the
fastest-closing offers (Festival Strategy, $50 Question) for the final week — a new
Sprint sold this late won't fully collect within 30 days anyway.

## Week 4 (Days 22–30): Close the gap, collect, document

**Day 22–24** — Objective: final push on Festival Strategy + Ask a Pro to any
still-unconverted engaged contacts; Advertise follow-ups with any B2B prospects from
earlier outreach.

**Day 25–27** — Objective: collect outstanding Sprint final payments from engagements
sold early in the month; chase any pending applications/proposals.

**Day 28–29** — Objective: last-call messaging — honest, not manipulative ("last
reminder this offer/price applies this month") to warm/relevant segments who showed
interest but haven't converted.

**Day 30** — Objective: close the books. Founder + AI: final `DASHBOARD.md` update,
compare actual vs. probable/weak/best case in `REVENUE-MODEL.md`, and write the Day 30
retrospective (below) as the input to the next 30-day cycle.

## Pivot rules (apply throughout, not just at week boundaries)

Change **one variable at a time**:
- Zero replies after a well-targeted send of 100+ → **channel/deliverability problem**:
  check spam placement, sender reputation, list staleness — not a copy problem yet.
- Replies/opens exist but no sales after 30+ qualified prospects for one offer →
  **offer or price problem** for that specific offer — revisit price framing or what's
  included, per that offer's objection-handling section.
- Prospects acknowledge the problem in conversation but won't commit to paying →
  **urgency/credibility problem** — tighten the specific outcome/timeline language,
  don't just discount.
- One channel/segment consistently outperforms another → reallocate outreach volume
  toward it immediately, don't wait for the weekly review to shift Week+1 sends.
- By Day 21, cumulative revenue is under 50% of the Day-21 benchmark ($4,000) →
  this is the stop-and-reassess trigger: the probable-case model's core assumption
  (list responsiveness) is wrong, not just under-executed. At that point, the right
  move is a founder-led root-cause review, not another send of the same campaigns.

## Day 30 retrospective template

- Actual revenue collected vs. best/probable/weak case
- Which offer over/under-performed its target, and by how much
- Which channel (warm 1:1 vs. broad email vs. referral) produced the best $/hour of
  founder effort
- What changed between Week 1 message and Week 4 message, and did it help
- Recommended offer/price/channel changes for the next 30-day cycle
- Whether reopening Funding Lab ($297) is now justified by demonstrated demand for
  a lower-touch version of the Sprint content
