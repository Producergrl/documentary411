# 4-Email Brand Outreach Sequence — Template

This is the client-facing deliverable: a customizable sequence the filmmaker sends (in
their own voice) to brand/sponsorship contacts from their 25-brand target list. Swap
brackets per recipient; keep the beat structure (specific hook → concrete ask → proof →
final low-friction close).

## Email 1 — First touch (send to a named contact, never "Sponsorships Team")

**Subject:** [Film Title] + [Brand] — a fit worth 15 minutes?

> Hi [Name],
>
> I'm [your name], [director/producer] of [Film Title], a documentary about
> [one-sentence logline]. I'm reaching out because [Brand]'s work in [specific
> category/campaign/value] overlaps directly with our film's audience: [name the
> specific audience].
>
> [One sentence of proof: festival selection, press, prior credit, or attached talent.]
>
> I'd like to send over a one-page overview and see if there's a fit for [Brand] to
> partner on the film's [funding/premiere/activation — be specific]. Worth 15 minutes
> on a call, or should I just send the one-pager first?
>
> [Your name]
> [Film website/socials]

## Email 2 — Follow-up with the one-pager (Day 4–5 if no reply)

**Subject:** (reply to same thread) — following up + the one-pager

> Following up with the one-pager I mentioned, attached. The short version: [audience]
> at [rough size/reach if known], a completed [or near-complete] film, and a specific
> partnership ask of [$ range] in exchange for [deliverables].
>
> Happy to walk through it on a quick call if useful.

## Email 3 — Value-add follow-up, not a repeat ask (Day 10–12)

**Subject:** thought you'd want to see this

> Not following up to push — wanted to share [a genuinely relevant update: a festival
> acceptance, a trailer view milestone, a piece of press, a relevant news hook tied to
> the film's subject]. Still think there's a real fit here whenever the timing works on
> your end.

## Email 4 — Final, low-friction close (Day 20–25)

**Subject:** closing the loop

> I know timing/budget cycles are real — wanted to close the loop rather than keep this
> sitting in your inbox. If [Brand] isn't the right fit right now, no worries at all,
> and I'd welcome a redirect to anyone else on your team (or elsewhere) who might see
> it differently. If it *is* worth a conversation, I'm still glad to make time.

---
**Cadence:** ~5 business days between touches. Track replies/no-replies per contact in
a simple sheet; stop after Email 4 unless the recipient re-engages.
