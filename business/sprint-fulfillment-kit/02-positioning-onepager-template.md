# Positioning One-Pager Template

One page, designed, handed to the client as both a working reference and something they
can forward to a warm contact ahead of a pitch call. Fill in every bracket; delete any
section that doesn't apply rather than leaving it generic.

---

**[FILM TITLE]**
*[One-sentence logline]*

**The story:** [3–4 sentences — what happens in the film, in plain, specific language.
No festival-blurb abstraction ("a journey of discovery") — name the actual people,
place, and stakes.]

**Why now:** [1–2 sentences — the real-world hook that makes this timely: an
anniversary, a policy moment, a cultural conversation already happening, a completion
date tied to a real event.]

**The audience:** [Name the specific community/demographic/interest group this reaches
— not "documentary lovers," but e.g. "competitive rock climbers and the outdoor
recreation industry that serves them."]

**Where that audience already is:** [3–5 real spaces — publications, retailers, events,
platforms, associations — this audience already engages with. This is the bridge to
the brand pitch: it's the same list a sponsor's marketing team would recognize.]

**What a brand partner gets:**
- Association with a completed, credible piece of storytelling (not a speculative ask)
- Direct access to [audience] through premiere events, community screenings, and
  social/press amplification
- [Any specific activation opportunity: co-branded screening series, panel discussion,
  behind-the-scenes content, talent appearances]

**Proof of credibility:**
- [Prior festival selections/awards, if any]
- [Filmmaker's relevant prior credits]
- [Any press already secured or in progress]
- [Any names attached — cast, executive producers, distribution/broadcast interest]

**The ask:** [Specific — e.g. "$25,000–$75,000 in production/completion funding in
exchange for [defined deliverables]" — not an open-ended "any support helps."]

**Contact:** [Name, email, phone] · [Film website/socials]

---
*Design note: this becomes a single branded PDF page in the client's own visual
identity (not Documentary411's) — use their poster art, trailer stills, or key art as
the visual anchor if available.*
