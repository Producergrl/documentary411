# Brand Category Target Map

Working list of brand categories that plausibly fund, sponsor, or partner on
independent documentaries — organized by the audience/values overlap that makes the
pitch make sense, not alphabetically. Use this to build each client's specific
25-brand target list: pick the 4–6 categories that genuinely match the film's audience
and world (from the intake questionnaire), then research 3–5 real, currently-active
companies per category.

**Every entry needs real, current research before it goes on a client's list** — this
map tells you where to look, it is not itself a verified contact list. Company
landscapes shift; verify each brand's current CSR/sponsorship activity before including
it.

## Outdoor, adventure & sustainability
1. Outdoor apparel & gear
2. Adventure travel & expedition companies
3. Footwear (hiking/trail/performance)
4. Outdoor gear rental/resale platforms
5. National park & conservation-adjacent commercial brands
6. Renewable energy (solar/wind)
7. EV & EV charging companies
8. Sustainable packaging/consumer goods brands
9. Environmental nonprofits with corporate-partnership arms

## Health, wellness & advocacy
10. Health & wellness apps/platforms
11. Mental health apps and services
12. Women's health brands
13. Fitness/athleisure brands
14. Health systems & hospital foundations (patient-story fit)
15. Biotech/pharma patient-advocacy programs
16. Veteran-support organizations

## Finance, insurance & professional services
17. Credit unions & community banks
18. Fintech/DTC banking brands
19. Insurance companies (CSR/community programs)
20. Law firms with impact-litigation or pro bono practices
21. Family offices / private foundations with media interests

## Food, beverage & hospitality
22. Coffee & tea brands
23. Beverage alcohol (spirits, wine, craft beer) with brand-story budgets
24. Restaurant groups & culinary brands
25. Farm-to-table / regenerative agriculture brands
26. Regional tourism boards & destination marketing organizations
27. Boutique hotel groups & luxury travel brands
28. Airlines with regional/sustainability marketing programs

## Retail, apparel & consumer brands
29. Heritage denim/workwear brands
30. Action sports & extreme sports brands
31. Beauty & personal care brands with mission-driven positioning
32. DTC subscription brands
33. Home goods & real estate/homebuilder brands (community-story fit)

## Media, tech & entertainment-adjacent
34. Streaming platforms with original-content acquisition arms (distinct from a
    funding ask — usually a later-stage conversation, not Day-1 outreach)
35. Camera & production-gear manufacturers (brands sponsor docs made with their own gear)
36. Audio/music tech brands
37. Gaming companies with narrative/story interests
38. Telecom companies with community/content marketing programs

## Automotive & mobility
39. Automotive brands with sustainability or heritage storytelling programs
40. Motorcycle/powersports brands
41. Rideshare/mobility platforms

## Sports & recreation
42. Professional sports leagues/teams (community relations arms)
43. Sports equipment manufacturers
44. Youth sports organizations with corporate sponsors attached

## Education & civic
45. University & college foundations
46. EdTech companies
47. Faith-based foundations
48. Civic/government-adjacent foundations (arts councils, humanities councils)

## Corporate social responsibility (CSR) divisions
49. Large retailers' CSR/foundation arms
50. Airlines' community/sustainability programs
51. Big Tech philanthropic/media arms
52. Consumer packaged goods (CPG) companies' cause-marketing budgets

## Craft, artisan & regional
53. Craft/artisan goods brands with strong brand narratives
54. Regional/local business associations and chambers of commerce
55. Wine & spirits estates with tourism/story budgets
56. Cruise lines (destination-documentary fit)

## Impact & mission-aligned funders (brand-adjacent, not pure grants)
57. B-Corps and mission-driven companies broadly
58. Impact investors with media/storytelling interest
59. Corporate foundations tied to the film's specific subject matter (identify per film)
60. Trade associations tied to the film's subject industry

---
*Precedent already cited on Documentary411's own site: Patagonia, REI, and Airbnb have
all funded or prominently supported independent documentaries — useful as a credibility
reference in client conversations, not as an implication that these specific companies
are currently soliciting new projects.*
