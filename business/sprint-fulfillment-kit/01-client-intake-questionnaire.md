# Funding Sprint — Client Intake Questionnaire

Send within 24 hours of deposit. Due back at least 48 hours before the positioning call
so there's time to review before you're on Zoom together.

## The film

1. Title (and any alternate/working titles)
2. One-sentence logline
3. Current stage (picture lock / rough cut / fine cut / still shooting) and target
   completion date
4. Runtime (actual or projected)
5. Link(s) to trailer, teaser, or best available cut
6. Link to any existing deck, one-pager, or pitch materials
7. Director/producer bios (2–3 sentences each) and any notable prior credits or awards

## Audience & world

8. Who is this film *for*, specifically? (Avoid "everyone who cares about the issue" —
   name the actual community, hobby, profession, or identity group.)
9. What real-world spaces does this audience already spend time/money in? (Brands,
   retailers, publications, events, platforms)
10. What is the film's tone — and what existing brand campaigns or other documentaries
    does it feel most similar to?

## Prior funding & outreach

11. What funding is already secured (amount + source), and what gap remains?
12. Have you approached any brands, sponsors, or foundations already? Who, and what
    happened?
13. Any existing relationships (personal or professional) with brand marketing,
    corporate social responsibility, or foundation contacts worth building on?

## Distribution & timeline

14. Target premiere/release window
15. Festival strategy so far, if any
16. Any distribution conversations already in progress

## Logistics

17. Best contact email and phone for scheduling
18. Time zone and 3–5 windows for the positioning call over the next two weeks
19. Anything else we should know before we build your package?

---
*Internal note: flag on intake if the answers to Q1–7 suggest the film isn't yet in a
fundable state (no cut, no clear audience, no logline) — raise it on the positioning
call rather than building a deck around a film that isn't ready. That's a scope
conversation, not a reason to withhold the deliverables already paid for.*
