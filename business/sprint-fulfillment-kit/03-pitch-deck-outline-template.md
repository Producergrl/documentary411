# 12-Slide Sponsor Pitch Deck — Outline Template

Structure used for every Funding Sprint client. Customize content per film; keep the
sequence — it's built to answer a brand marketer's questions in the order they actually
ask them (story → audience → proof → ask), not the order a filmmaker wants to tell it.

1. **Title** — Film title, one-line logline, key art/still, filmmaker credit line
2. **The story** — 3–4 sentences, specific and concrete, not a festival blurb. Include a
   striking still or trailer-frame.
3. **Why this film, why now** — the real-world hook (anniversary, cultural moment,
   completion timeline) that creates urgency for a partner to act this cycle, not "someday"
4. **Meet the filmmakers** — bios, relevant prior credits/awards, any attached talent
5. **The audience** — named specifically (see positioning one-pager); include any real
   data available (community size, platform following, prior project reach)
6. **Where this audience already spends attention** — the bridge slide: publications,
   retailers, events, associations this audience already engages with, framed as
   "this is where your brand already wants to be"
7. **Why [Brand Category] specifically** — tailored per prospect type; the client's own
   copy of this deck should have 2–3 swappable versions of this slide for their top
   brand categories from the target list
8. **Proof of execution** — prior festival selections, press, distribution history, cut
   quality (a strong 60–90 second trailer link belongs here if the deck is presented live)
9. **What partnership includes** — concrete deliverables for the brand: premiere access,
   co-branded content, panel/activation opportunities, credit placement, usage rights if
   applicable
10. **Budget & use of funds** — enough specificity to be credible (post-production,
    festival strategy, impact campaign) without a full line-item budget
11. **The ask** — a specific number or range, tied to specific deliverables — never
    open-ended
12. **Contact & next step** — names, contact info, and one clear proposed next action
    ("15-minute call to discuss fit")

## Design notes for the client's version

- Match the film's own visual identity (poster, key art, color palette) — never
  Documentary411 branding
- One idea per slide; the deck should be legible without narration
- Keep slide 7 as a living template — the same deck gets a swapped-in slide 7 for each
  of the 25 brands on the target list where the pitch is tailored, not identical copies
  sent everywhere
