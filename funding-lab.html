Brand-Funded Documentary

The Grants Are Gone. The Brands Are Not.

A step-by-step system for funding your documentary with brand and sponsor money — built by producers who've actually done it.

In 2025, Congress rescinded the entire $1.1 billion public-broadcasting budget. ITVS — the org behind $40M+ in doc funding — lost 86% of its support. The IDA paused its Enterprise Documentary Fund. If your funding plan is "apply for grants and hope," 2026 already broke it.

Meanwhile, something else happened: brands started funding documentaries at a pace nobody predicted. Marketers now commission films, premiere them, and pay real budgets for them — because a great documentary buys a brand more trust than any ad. (Ask Patagonia, REI, Airbnb, and a hundred regional brands you've never thought to pitch.)

The money didn't disappear. It moved. This is the system for following it.
