Not a grant, still useful

Fiscal sponsorship resources for documentary filmmakers.

Fiscal sponsorship can help a film receive tax-deductible donations and apply to certain foundations, but it is not the same thing as receiving grant money. Documentary411 labels this clearly.

Funding infrastructure

Fiscal Sponsorship

Use this when a project needs nonprofit funding access, donor confidence, or foundation eligibility. Always verify fees, reporting obligations, eligibility, and whether the sponsor is the right fit for your film.
